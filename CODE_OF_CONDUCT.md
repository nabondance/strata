# Code of conduct

Strata follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where the project is represented.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, accept constructive criticism, and focus on what is best for the people using and building Strata. Harassment, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, and publishing someone's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainer, [@nabondance](https://github.com/nabondance), through the contact details on that GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue for a conduct report. Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove or edit comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor, following the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
